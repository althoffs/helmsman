GPS needs an HTTPS connection. Switch GPS on and allow location access when Safari asks.

SOG and COG are measured by GPS over ground. Heading is the bow direction measured by the device (held upright or in the hand, back facing forward), corrected by the magnetic variation you set. The compass dial rotates, the white mark at the top is the bow, the amber marker shows the GPS course (COG), the red diamond shows the bearing to the selected waypoint. Drift is the difference between COG and heading (STBD = to starboard, PORT = to port) and is only shown above about 1 kn. The device compass can be disturbed on board by metal and electrical equipment. MOB only saves the position where the button was pressed, not the location of the person. Map data needs an internet connection. HELMSMAN is a supplementary instrument and no substitute for proper navigation and rescue equipment.
