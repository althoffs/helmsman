SOG and COG are measured by GPS over ground.

Heading is the bow direction measured by the device (held upright or in the hand, back facing forward), corrected by the magnetic variation you set.

The compass dial rotates, the white mark at the top is the bow, the amber marker shows the GPS course (COG), the green diamond the bearing to the selected waypoint (red for MOB), the blue marker the direction the wind comes from.

Drift is the difference between COG and heading (STBD = to starboard, PORT = to port) and is only shown above about 1 kn.

Weather values are model forecasts for your position and a point ahead on your course (Open-Meteo), not measurements, and they need an internet connection.

The device compass can be disturbed on board by metal and electrical equipment.

MOB only saves the position where the button was pressed, not the location of the person.

Map data needs an internet connection.

The small Sea Map on Instruments only shows where you are. On both maps the blue cone shows the direction the device points (compass needed).

Power Saver slows the compass and map updates, stops the pulsing and dims the screen after 20 seconds without touch, but never while a MOB is active.

HELMSMAN is a supplementary instrument and no substitute for proper navigation, weather and rescue equipment.
